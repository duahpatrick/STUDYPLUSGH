Internet Safety

Protect your identity, devices and relationships while using the internet responsibly.

Key idea

Smart online choices keep you, your information and your reputation safe.

Protecting Personal Information

Personal information includes name, email, phone, address, school, payment information and passwords. Never share this with strangers online. Even trusted sites can be hacked.

Strong passwords: at least 12 characters, mix uppercase, lowercase, numbers and symbols. Use different passwords for different websites. Enable two-factor authentication where available.

Recognising Online Threats

Phishing: fake emails asking for login details. Malware: harmful programs that damage devices or steal information. Ransomware: locks files until money is paid. Scams: offers that seem too good to be true usually are.

Warning signs: unexpected emails asking to "verify" your account, offers of free money or prizes, links you don't recognize.

Safe Online Behaviour

Think before posting: anything online can be permanent. Don't share embarrassing photos or private conversations. Don't click links from unknown sources. Don't download files unless you know they're safe.

Use privacy settings on social media. Be kind online; cyberbullying causes real harm. Report abuse to platform moderators and trusted adults.

Device Security

Keep software updated (updates include security fixes). Use antivirus software and run regular scans. Create backups of important files. Lock your device with a password or fingerprint.

What to Do If Something Goes Wrong

If you receive threatening messages, tell a trusted adult immediately. If you think you've been hacked, change passwords and alert the service. If you've shared personal info by accident, contact the company's support team.

Report cyberbullying to the platform and to school authorities.

Quick Check

What should you do if you receive an unexpected email asking you to confirm your password?

Do not click the link or reply. This is likely phishing. Contact the company directly using their official website.
