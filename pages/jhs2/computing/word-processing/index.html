JHS 2 Computing

Word Processing

Create, format and edit professional documents for school and everyday communication.

Key idea

Word processors combine writing, design and organisation tools to produce clear, attractive documents.

Starting a Document

Before opening the software, plan your document's purpose, audience and format. Think about what you need to say, who you are saying it to, and what structure works best.

Planning a letter: purpose is to request information, audience is a business, format is formal with letterhead and date.

Formatting Text

Apply font, size, colour and style (bold, italic, underline). Use headings to organise sections. Add line spacing and margins for readability. Align text left, centre or right as needed.

Use formatting consistently: keep headings the same style throughout, maintain consistent spacing between sections.

Using Lists and Tables

Bullet points and numbered lists make information clear and organised. Tables show data in rows and columns, making comparisons easy.

A numbered list works well for steps. A table works well for showing prices and quantities.

Checking and Saving

Before finishing, use spell-check to find errors. Proofread manually for grammar and sense. Check that formatting is consistent. Save your work frequently with a descriptive filename.

Printing and Sharing

Preview your document before printing to check formatting. Save in multiple formats (PDF for sharing, DOCX for editing). Keep a backup on cloud storage.

Quick Check

Why should you plan a document before opening the software?

Planning helps you organise your ideas, choose the right format, and create a more effective document.
