Fractions, Decimals and Percentages

Move confidently between three useful ways of describing parts of a whole.

Key idea

Fractions, decimals, and percentages can represent the same value in different forms.

Equivalent Forms

A fraction compares a part with a whole. A decimal uses place value, while a percentage describes a quantity out of 100.

1/2 = 0.5 = 50%

Converting Fractions

To change a fraction to a decimal, divide the numerator by the denominator. To change it to a percentage, multiply the decimal by 100.

3/4 = 0.75

0.75 × 100 = 75%

Percentages of Quantities

Change the percentage to a fraction or decimal, then multiply by the quantity.

20% of 60 = 0.20 × 60 = 12

Comparing Values

Convert values to the same form before comparing them. This prevents mistakes when the denominators or place values look different.

0.6 = 60% and 3/5 = 60%, so all three values are equal.

Worked Example

Find 25% of eighty.

25% = 0.25. Then 0.25 × 80 = 20.

Remember

Look for the whole, choose a useful form, and check whether your answer is reasonable.
