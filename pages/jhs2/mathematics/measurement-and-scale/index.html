Measurement and Scale

Measure real-world objects, convert units, and use scale to represent larger spaces.

Key idea

Good measurement depends on the correct unit, accurate conversion, and careful interpretation of the problem.

Common Units

Length is measured in millimetres, centimetres, metres, and kilometres. Mass uses grams and kilograms. Capacity uses millilitres and litres.

1 m = 100 cm   |   1 kg = 1,000 g   |   1 L = 1,000 mL

Converting Units

Move to a smaller unit by multiplying. Move to a larger unit by dividing.

4.5 m = 450 cm

2,500 g = 2.5 kg

Perimeter and Area

Perimeter is the distance around a shape. Area is the amount of surface inside it.

Rectangle perimeter = 2(l + w); rectangle area = l × w.

Scale Drawings

A scale compares a drawing distance with the real distance. Keep both distances in matching units before calculating.

At a scale of 1 cm : 5 m, 3 cm represents 15 m.

Worked Example

Find the area of a rectangle 8 cm long and 5 cm wide.

Area = length × width = 8 × 5 = 40 cm².

Remember

Write the unit with every measurement, convert before calculating, and check that the answer fits the situation.
