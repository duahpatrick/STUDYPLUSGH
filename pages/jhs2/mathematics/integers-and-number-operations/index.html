Integers and Number Operations

Use positive and negative numbers confidently in calculations and everyday situations.

Key idea

Integer rules help us compare, add, subtract, multiply, and divide numbers on either side of zero.

What Are Integers?

Integers are whole numbers, their opposites, and zero. They include ..., -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3, ... but not fractions or decimals.

On a number line, numbers increase as we move right and decrease as we move left.

Comparing Integers

A positive integer is greater than zero. A negative integer is less than zero. Among negative numbers, the number closer to zero is greater.

5 > -2

-3 > -8

-6 < 1

Adding and Subtracting

When signs are the same, add the absolute values and keep the sign. When signs differ, subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger and use the sign of the larger.

-7 + 4 = -3   |   6 - (-2) = 6 + 2 = 8

Multiplying and Dividing

Two numbers with the same sign give a positive answer. Two numbers with different signs give a negative answer.

(-4) × (-3) = 12

(-20) ÷ 5 = -4

Worked Example

Calculate -8 + 3 - (-4).

-8 + 3 = -5. Subtracting -4 means adding 4: -5 + 4 = -1.

Remember

Use a number line when signs feel confusing. Keep track of each sign, especially when subtracting a negative number.
