Vocabulary and Word Study Practice

JHS 2 English

Question 1

Give a synonym for rapid.

Answer: Quick or fast.



Question 2

Give an antonym for ancient.

Answer: Modern or new.



Question 3

What does unhappy mean? Identify the prefix.

Answer: Not happy; the prefix is un-.



Question 4

In “The glass was fragile, so Ama carried it carefully,” what does fragile mean?

Answer: Easily broken. The careful carrying is the context clue.



Question 5

Add a suffix to teach to name a person who teaches.

Answer: Teacher; the suffix is -er.
