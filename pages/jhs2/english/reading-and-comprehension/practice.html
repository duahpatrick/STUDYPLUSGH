How to Use This Page

Read each passage twice. Try the questions before looking at the solved answers. Then compare your response with the model answer and the evidence from the passage.

Passage 1: The Reading Corner

The library at Nhyira JHS had many useful books, but few students visited it after lessons. The shelves were crowded, and there was no quiet place for students to read. During a class discussion, Efua suggested creating a reading corner near the windows. Her classmates agreed to help.

They sorted the books, repaired torn covers, and arranged the shelves by subject. The carpenter in the community donated two benches, while parents provided bright curtains. The English teacher made a weekly reading timetable so every class could use the corner.

Within a month, more students began visiting the library. Some read storybooks, while others used reference books for assignments. The project showed the students that a shared space can improve when people notice a problem and work together to solve it.

Questions on Passage 1

1. Why did Efua suggest creating a reading corner?

2. Name two things the students did to improve the library.

3. Who donated the benches?

4. What can we infer about the community?

5. What is the main lesson of the passage?

6. Find a word in the passage that means sources used to find information.

Solved Answers: Passage 1

1. Answer: Efua suggested it because the library had useful books but few visitors, crowded shelves, and no quiet reading space.

2. Answer: They sorted the books, repaired torn covers, and arranged the shelves by subject. Any two are correct.

3. Answer: A carpenter in the community donated the benches.

4. Answer: We can infer that the community was supportive and willing to help the school improve.

5. Answer: People can improve a shared space when they notice a problem and work together to solve it.

6. Answer: The words are reference books. They provide information for study and assignments.

Passage 2: The Missing Notebook

On the morning of the history test, Kofi discovered that his notebook was missing. He searched his desk, his schoolbag, and the classroom cupboard, but it was nowhere to be found. He felt worried because the notebook contained the notes he needed for revision.

Instead of blaming another student, Kofi retraced his steps from the previous afternoon. He remembered reading under the mango tree before football practice. When he reached the tree, he found the notebook beneath a bench. A strong wind had blown several pages open, but the writing was still safe.

Kofi thanked the students who had helped him search. He then placed the notebook in a labelled folder inside his bag. From that day, he checked his belongings before leaving school and kept important work in one safe place.

Questions on Passage 2

1. Why was Kofi worried?

2. Where did Kofi eventually find the notebook?

3. How did Kofi search for the missing notebook?

4. What can we infer about Kofi's character?

5. What does retraced his steps mean?

6. Suggest a suitable title for the passage and explain your choice.

Solved Answers: Passage 2

1. Answer: He was worried because the notebook contained the notes he needed to revise for the history test.

2. Answer: He found it beneath a bench under the mango tree.

3. Answer: He searched his desk, schoolbag, and classroom cupboard, then retraced his steps from the previous afternoon.

4. Answer: We can infer that Kofi is calm, responsible, and willing to solve a problem carefully instead of blaming others.

5. Answer: It means he went back over the places he had visited earlier.

6. Answer: Finding the Missing Notebook is suitable because the passage describes Kofi's search and the lesson he learned about caring for his belongings.
