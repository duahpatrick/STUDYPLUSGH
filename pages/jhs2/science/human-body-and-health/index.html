Human Body and Health

Understand major body systems and make choices that support physical and mental well-being.

Key idea

Body systems work together, and healthy choices support their function and your overall well-being.

Major Body Systems

Circulatory: heart, blood vessels, blood (carries oxygen). Respiratory: lungs, airways (takes in oxygen). Digestive: mouth, stomach, intestines (breaks down food). Nervous: brain, nerves (processes information). Muscular and skeletal: bones, muscles (structure and movement).

Nutrition and Diet

Macronutrients: carbohydrates (energy), proteins (growth and repair), fats (energy and protection). Micronutrients: vitamins and minerals (body functions). A balanced diet includes all food groups in correct proportions.

Common deficiency issues: lack of iron causes anemia, lack of iodine affects thyroid.

Exercise and Fitness

Regular exercise strengthens heart and lungs, builds muscle, maintains healthy weight and improves mood. Aerobic exercise (running, swimming) builds endurance. Strength training builds muscle. Flexibility exercises improve range of motion.

Disease Prevention and Immunity

Pathogens (bacteria, viruses) cause disease. The immune system fights infection. Vaccination teaches immune system to recognize threats. Hygiene (handwashing, sanitation) prevents spread of disease.

Washing hands removes pathogens. Vaccines train the body's defenses without causing illness.

Mental Health and Stress

Mental health is as important as physical health. Stress is normal but chronic stress harms health. Managing stress: exercise, talk to someone, practise relaxation techniques. Sleep, social connection and hobbies support mental well-being.

Quick Check

What are macronutrients and why do we need them?

Macronutrients are carbohydrates, proteins and fats. They provide energy, support growth and body functions.
