Matter and Energy

Understand the properties of matter and how energy transfers and transforms.

Key idea

Matter has measurable properties, and energy flows through systems causing changes.

Properties of Matter

Density: how much mass is packed into a volume. Hardness: resistance to scratching or denting. Melting point: temperature at which solid becomes liquid. Boiling point: temperature at which liquid becomes gas.

These properties help identify substances and predict how they behave.

Changes of State

Melting: solid → liquid (heat added). Freezing: liquid → solid (heat removed). Evaporation: liquid → gas (heat added). Condensation: gas → liquid (heat removed). Sublimation: solid → gas directly.

Ice melting into water, water boiling into steam, frost forming without liquid water.

Forms of Energy

Thermal (heat): energy from particle movement. Kinetic: energy of motion. Potential: stored energy. Light: travels as waves. Sound: travels through air and materials. Electrical: flow of electrons.

Energy Transfer and Transformation

Energy transfers from one place to another. Energy transforms from one type to another. Total energy in a system remains constant (conservation of energy). When a ball falls, potential energy becomes kinetic energy.

Heat and Temperature

Temperature measures how fast particles move. Heat is energy that flows from hot to cold. Adding heat increases temperature. Heat always flows from hot to cold, never the reverse.

Quick Check

What is the difference between heat and temperature?

Temperature measures how fast particles move. Heat is the energy that flows from hot to cold.
