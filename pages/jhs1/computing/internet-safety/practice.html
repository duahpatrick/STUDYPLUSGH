JHS 1 Computing

10 Practice Questions

1. What is personal information? Answer: Details that identify you.

2. Name one private detail. Answer: Password or home address.

3. Should passwords be shared with friends? Answer: No.

4. What is cyberbullying? Answer: Bullying through digital technology.

5. Who should receive a safety report? Answer: A trusted adult or platform.

6. Give one sign of a scam. Answer: An urgent request for secrets or money.

7. Why update software? Answer: To improve security and fix faults.

8. Should unknown links be clicked? Answer: No.

9. What should you do before posting? Answer: Think about safety and respect.

10. What is two-step verification? Answer: An extra account security check.
