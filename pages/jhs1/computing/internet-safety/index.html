Internet Safety

Learn habits that keep your accounts, devices and relationships safer online.

Personal Information

Personal information includes passwords, home address, phone number and school details. Share it only with trusted adults or approved services.


Use strong, unique passwords and enable two-step verification where available.



Online Behaviour

Think before posting, be respectful and do not forward harmful rumours or private images. Cyberbullying should be reported to a trusted adult and the platform.



Scams and Malware

Unexpected links, urgent prizes and requests for passwords may be scams. Keep software updated, use trusted sources and ask an adult before downloading.


Remember

Stop, think, check and report when something online feels unsafe.

Quick Check

Should you share your password with a friend?

No. Keep it private and ask a trusted adult for help.
