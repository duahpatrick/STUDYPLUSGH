Computer Systems

Understand the parts that make a computer useful.

Hardware and Software

Hardware is the physical part of a computer that can be seen and touched. Software is a set of instructions that tells hardware what to do.


A keyboard is hardware; a word processor is software.



People and Processing

Users provide data and instructions. The computer processes data, stores results and produces information. The CPU carries out most processing operations.



Care and Maintenance

Keep equipment clean and dry, shut down correctly, protect cables and report faults. Never force a connector into the wrong port.


Remember

Hardware needs software, and both need a responsible user.

Quick Check

Is a monitor hardware or software?
