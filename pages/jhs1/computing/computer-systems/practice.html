Computer Systems Practice

JHS 1 Computing

10 Practice Questions

1. Define hardware. Answer: Physical computer parts.

2. Define software. Answer: Instructions used by a computer.

3. Is a keyboard hardware? Answer: Yes.

4. What does the CPU do? Answer: It processes instructions.

5. Who is a user? Answer: A person who operates a computer.

6. Give one software example. Answer: Word processor.

7. Why keep equipment dry? Answer: Moisture can damage it.

8. What should you do with a fault? Answer: Report it.

9. What is data? Answer: Raw facts entered for processing.

10. Name one output device. Answer: Monitor or printer.
