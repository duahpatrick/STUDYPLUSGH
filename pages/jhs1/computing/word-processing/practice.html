Word Processing Practice

JHS 1 Computing

10 Practice Questions

1. What is word processing? Answer: Creating and editing text documents.

2. Why use headings? Answer: To organise ideas.

3. What does bold change? Answer: Makes text darker and heavier.

4. What does editing change? Answer: The content.

5. What does formatting change? Answer: The appearance.

6. Why save a document? Answer: To keep the work.

7. What is proofreading? Answer: Checking for errors.

8. Name one alignment. Answer: Left, centre or right.

9. What can bullets organise? Answer: A list of items.

10. Why acknowledge sources? Answer: To respect ideas and avoid plagiarism.
