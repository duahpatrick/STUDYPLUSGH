JHS 1 Computing

10 Practice Questions

1. Define algorithm. Answer: Ordered steps for solving a problem.

2. Why must steps be ordered? Answer: Correct order supports the result.

3. What is a problem? Answer: A task or challenge needing a solution.

4. Should an algorithm have a finish? Answer: Yes.

5. What does clear mean? Answer: Easy to understand without confusion.

6. Why test an algorithm? Answer: To find and correct errors.

7. Give one daily algorithm. Answer: Steps for preparing a drink.

8. What is an instruction? Answer: A direction to perform an action.

9. What should you do before writing steps? Answer: Understand the problem.

10. What is an error in an algorithm? Answer: A step that causes an incorrect result.
