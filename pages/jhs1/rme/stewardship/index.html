Stewardship

Care for people, property and the natural world through responsible choices.

Meaning of Stewardship

Stewardship is responsible management and care of something entrusted to us. It includes time, talents, money, public property, animals and natural resources.



Practical Care

Use water carefully, avoid littering, protect school property, plant trees and share skills to help others. Stewardship also means using talents for constructive purposes.


Example

Switching off lights in an empty classroom saves energy and shows care for shared resources.



Remember

Good stewardship protects today’s resources and leaves a better world for tomorrow.

Quick Check

Is leaving taps running good stewardship?

No. Turn taps off when they are not needed.
