Stewardship Practice

JHS 1 RME

10 Practice Questions

1. Define stewardship. Answer: Responsible care of what is entrusted to us.

2. Name one thing people steward. Answer: Time, talents, property or resources.

3. Why switch off unused lights? Answer: To save energy.

4. How can water be conserved? Answer: Close unused taps.

5. Why protect school property? Answer: It is shared by learners.

6. What does littering cause? Answer: An untidy and unhealthy environment.

7. Why plant trees? Answer: To protect soil and nature.

8. How can talents serve others? Answer: Use skills to help constructively.

9. What does responsible use mean? Answer: Avoiding waste and damage.

10. Who should practise stewardship? Answer: Everyone.
