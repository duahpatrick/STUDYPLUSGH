Moral Values

Good values help people make fair, wise and caring choices.

Important Values

Honesty means speaking truthfully. Respect means treating people and property with care. Kindness means responding to others’ needs. Self-control means managing feelings and actions wisely.



Applying Values

Values matter most when choices are difficult. Returning lost money, apologising after harm and refusing to cheat are examples of moral action.


Decision steps

Pause, consider who may be affected, choose the fair action and accept responsibility.



Remember

Character is built by practising good choices consistently, even when no one is watching.

Quick Check
