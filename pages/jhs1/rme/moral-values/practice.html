Moral Values Practice

JHS 1 RME

10 Practice Questions

1. Define honesty. Answer: Speaking and acting truthfully.

2. Define respect. Answer: Treating people and property with care.

3. What is kindness? Answer: Responding helpfully to others.

4. What is self-control? Answer: Managing feelings and actions wisely.

5. Why return lost property? Answer: It is honest.

6. What should follow an apology? Answer: An effort to correct the harm.

7. How are values shown? Answer: Through everyday choices.

8. Why avoid cheating? Answer: It is dishonest and unfair.

9. What is fairness? Answer: Treating people justly.

10. When should good values be practised? Answer: At all times.
