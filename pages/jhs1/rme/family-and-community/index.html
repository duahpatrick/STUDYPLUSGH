Family and Community

Learn how service and peaceful relationships strengthen society.

Living with Others

Families and communities need love, fairness, patience and cooperation. People have different ages, abilities and opinions, but each person deserves dignity.



Service and Peace

Service is using time or ability to help others without expecting unfair reward. Peaceful conflict resolution includes listening, apologising, forgiveness and seeking help from trusted leaders.


Example

Helping an elderly neighbour carry water is an act of service.



Remember

Peace begins with respectful words, fair actions and willingness to help.

Quick Check

What is one peaceful way to solve disagreement?

Listen, discuss calmly and seek a fair solution.
