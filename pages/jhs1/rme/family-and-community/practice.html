Family and Community Practice

JHS 1 RME

10 Practice Questions

1. Why is family important? Answer: It provides care and support.

2. Name one quality needed in a family. Answer: Love, patience or fairness.

3. What is service? Answer: Helping others with time or ability.

4. Give one act of service. Answer: Helping an elderly neighbour.

5. What is peace? Answer: Living without harmful conflict.

6. How can people resolve conflict? Answer: Listen and discuss calmly.

7. Why forgive? Answer: It helps restore relationships.

8. What is dignity? Answer: The worth every person deserves.

9. Why cooperate? Answer: To achieve shared goals.

10. Name one peaceful action. Answer: Apologising or seeking help.
