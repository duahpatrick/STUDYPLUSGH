Worship and Prayer Practice

JHS 1 RME

10 Practice Questions

1. Define worship. Answer: Showing honour and devotion to God.

2. Define prayer. Answer: Communicating with God.

3. Name one form of worship. Answer: Singing, service, offering or reading sacred texts.

4. What is praise? Answer: Expressing honour and admiration.

5. What is thanksgiving? Answer: Expressing gratitude.

6. What is confession? Answer: Admitting wrong actions.

7. What is intercession? Answer: Praying for others.

8. Why respect another person’s practice? Answer: It is part of religious tolerance.

9. How should worship influence conduct? Answer: Encourage good behaviour.

10. Name one attitude prayer can develop. Answer: Humility or gratitude.
