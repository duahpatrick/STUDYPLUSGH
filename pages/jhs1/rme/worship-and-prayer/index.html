Worship and Prayer

Understand how people express faith and communicate with God.

Meaning of Worship

Worship is showing honour, love and devotion to God. It may include singing, reading sacred texts, offering, service, silence and gathering with others.



Meaning of Prayer

Prayer is communicating with God. It may include praise, thanksgiving, confession, asking for help and interceding for others.

Respectful learners should not mock another person’s sincere religious practice.



Remember

Worship should influence daily conduct by encouraging humility, gratitude and good treatment of others.

Quick Check

Name one part of prayer.

Praise, thanksgiving, confession, asking or intercession.
