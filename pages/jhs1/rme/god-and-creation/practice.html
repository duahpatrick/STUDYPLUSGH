God and Creation Practice

JHS 1 RME

10 Practice Questions

1. What is creation? Answer: The world and life brought into existence.

2. Name two parts of creation. Answer: Plants, animals, people, land, water or sky.

3. What do many traditions teach about God? Answer: God is creator and sustainer.

4. What is gratitude? Answer: Thankfulness.

5. Why should people care for nature? Answer: People depend on it.

6. Give one way to care for creation. Answer: Avoid littering or plant trees.

7. Why respect different beliefs? Answer: People hold sincere beliefs.

8. How does cleanliness show responsibility? Answer: It protects shared surroundings.

9. What is a resource? Answer: Something useful to people.

10. State one attitude creation should encourage. Answer: Gratitude or care.
