God and Creation

Learn how beliefs about creation encourage gratitude and responsibility.

Belief in God

Many religious traditions teach that God is the creator and sustainer of life. Beliefs may use different names and explanations, so learners should discuss them respectfully.


Creation includes human beings, animals, plants, land, water and the sky.



Human Responsibility

People depend on the natural world for food, water and shelter. This creates a duty to use resources wisely, protect life and avoid unnecessary destruction.


Example

Keeping a school compound clean shows gratitude and responsible care for creation.



Remember

Respect religious differences, appreciate life and care for the world entrusted to people.

Quick Check

What attitude should creation encourage?

Gratitude and responsible care.
