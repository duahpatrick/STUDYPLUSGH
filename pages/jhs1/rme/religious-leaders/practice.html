Religious Leaders Practice

JHS 1 RME

10 Practice Questions

1. Name one role of a religious leader. Answer: Teaching or guiding people.

2. What is counselling? Answer: Giving wise support and advice.

3. Why do leaders teach values? Answer: To guide behaviour.

4. Name one quality of a good leader. Answer: Wisdom, fairness or humility.

5. Why is integrity important? Answer: It makes leadership trustworthy.

6. What does compassion mean? Answer: Concern for others’ suffering.

7. How can leaders serve communities? Answer: Organise help and guidance.

8. Why respect different leaders? Answer: Traditions have different structures.

9. What should leaders avoid? Answer: Harmful and unfair treatment.

10. How do leaders lead by example? Answer: They practise the values they teach.
