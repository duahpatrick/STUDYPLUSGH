Religious Leaders

Study how leaders teach, guide and serve their communities.

Roles of Leaders

Religious leaders teach beliefs and moral values, lead worship, counsel people, settle disagreements and organise service. Examples of titles differ among traditions.



Qualities of Good Leadership

Good leaders show wisdom, humility, fairness, courage, compassion and integrity. They guide through example rather than demanding harmful treatment.


Respectful Learning

Different traditions may have different leaders and practices. Learn about them accurately and speak about them respectfully.



Remember

Leadership is a responsibility to guide, protect and serve people.

Quick Check

Which quality makes leadership trustworthy?

Integrity, or consistently doing what is right.
