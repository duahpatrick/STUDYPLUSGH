Vocabulary Practice

JHS 1 English Language

Question 1

Which is a synonym for “happy”? A. joyful B. angry C. tired D. narrow
Answer: A. joyful


Question 2

Which is an antonym for “ancient”? A. old B. historic C. modern D. early
Answer: C. modern


Question 3

What does “narrow” mean in “a narrow path”? A. not wide B. very long C. colourful D. noisy
Answer: A. not wide


Question 4

What does the prefix “un-” often mean? A. again B. not C. before D. small
Answer: B. not


Question 5

Which word is formed with a suffix? A. unhappy B. replay C. helpful D. dislike
Answer: C. helpful


Question 6

Choose the correct word: The baby slept __. A. peaceful B. peace C. peacefully D. peacefulness
Answer: C. peacefully


Question 7

Which pair are antonyms? A. fast/quick B. begin/start C. arrive/depart D. small/little
Answer: C. arrive/depart


Question 8

In “The glass is fragile, so handle it carefully,” fragile means __. A. easily broken B. very heavy C. expensive D. empty
Answer: A. easily broken


Question 9

Which word belongs to the family of “teach”? A. teacher B. table C. thought D. touch
Answer: A. teacher


Question 10

Which is the most formal word for “help”? A. assist B. fix C. aidy D. hand
Answer: A. assist
