Sentence Structure Practice

JHS 1 English Language

Question 1

A complete sentence expresses a __ thought. A. partial B. complete C. confusing D. silent
Answer: B. complete


Question 2

Which mark ends a question? A. . B. , C. ? D. !
Answer: C. ?


Question 3

Identify the subject: “The young farmer works hard.” A. works B. hard C. The young farmer D. farmer works
Answer: C. The young farmer


Question 4

“Please close the door” is what type? A. imperative B. interrogative C. exclamatory D. declarative
Answer: A. imperative


Question 5

Which is a compound sentence? A. Ama sings. B. Ama sings and Kojo dances. C. Singing Ama. D. Ama happy.
Answer: B. Ama sings and Kojo dances.


Question 6

Choose the correct verb: “The girls __ to school.” A. walks B. walk C. walking D. is walk
Answer: B. walk


Question 7

Which is declarative? A. Sit down. B. Where are you? C. The sun is hot. D. What a goal!
Answer: C. The sun is hot.


Question 8

Which word can join two ideas? A. and B. quickly C. under D. red
Answer: A. and


Question 9

Choose the correct sentence. A. He go home. B. He goes home. C. He going home. D. He gone home.
Answer: B. He goes home.


Question 10

What does the predicate tell us? A. who the sentence is about B. what the subject does or is C. the punctuation D. the title
Answer: B. what the subject does or is
