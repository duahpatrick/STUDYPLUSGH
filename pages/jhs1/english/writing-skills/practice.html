Writing Skills Practice

JHS 1 English Language

Question 1

What should you do before writing? A. Plan ideas B. Erase the topic C. Skip the audience D. Write randomly
Answer: A. Plan ideas


Question 2

What does a topic sentence introduce? A. The main idea B. The page number C. The writer's name D. The last detail
Answer: A. The main idea


Question 3

Which is a linking word? A. therefore B. pencil C. quickly D. blue
Answer: A. therefore


Question 4

Which belongs in a narrative? A. Characters B. A shopping price only C. A dictionary list D. A map scale
Answer: A. Characters


Question 5

Descriptive writing uses __ details. A. sensory B. silent C. numerical only D. unrelated
Answer: A. sensory


Question 6

What should a paragraph mainly develop? A. One main idea B. Ten unrelated ideas C. A title only D. A question only
Answer: A. One main idea


Question 7

Which is a suitable greeting for an informal letter? A. Dear Auntie Ama, B. Yours faithfully, C. Subject: D. Chapter One
Answer: A. Dear Auntie Ama,


Question 8

Why do writers edit? A. To improve errors B. To remove all ideas C. To hide the topic D. To shorten every word
Answer: A. To improve errors


Question 9

Which sentence is the best topic sentence about cleanliness? A. Cleanliness keeps our environment healthy. B. I saw a red pen. C. Saturday has seven letters. D. My shoes are size eight.
Answer: A. Cleanliness keeps our environment healthy.


Question 10

Which should be checked during editing? A. Spelling and punctuation B. The weather only C. The classroom colour D. The reader's height
Answer: A. Spelling and punctuation
