JHS 1 English

Writing Skills

Plan, organise and edit clear writing for school and everyday life.

Key idea

Strong writing starts with a clear plan, good structure, and careful editing.

Planning Before Writing

Read the topic carefully and identify its purpose, audience and format. Brainstorm ideas, select the strongest points and arrange them in a simple plan before drafting.



Paragraph Structure

A paragraph develops one main idea. Begin with a topic sentence, add supporting details or examples, and finish with a concluding sentence or link to the next idea.


Use linking words such as first, next, however, therefore and finally to guide the reader.



Descriptive and Narrative Writing

Descriptive writing helps the reader see, hear or feel something through precise adjectives and sensory details. Narrative writing tells events in order and includes setting, characters, actions and a clear ending.



Informal Letters and Editing

An informal letter includes an address and date, greeting, well-organised paragraphs, a closing and the writer's name. After drafting, check spelling, punctuation, capital letters, grammar and whether every sentence supports the topic.



Interactive Check

Which is the best topic sentence for a paragraph about keeping the school clean?

A. Yesterday was Monday. B. Keeping our school clean is everyone's responsibility.

B is best because it introduces the paragraph's main idea clearly.



Remember

Plan first, write for your audience, organise one main idea per paragraph, use linking words, and edit before submitting.
