Comprehension Practice

JHS 1 English Language

Passage

Every Saturday, Yaw helped his aunt at the community garden. He watered the vegetables, removed weeds and recorded the growth of the plants. After three months, the garden produced enough tomatoes and peppers to share with several families. Yaw learned that patient work can benefit a whole community.


Question 1

When did Yaw help at the garden? A. Every morning B. Every Saturday C. Every holiday D. Every evening
Answer: B. Every Saturday


Question 2

Name one task Yaw performed. A. Sold clothes B. Watered vegetables C. Built a house D. Cooked rice
Answer: B. Watered vegetables


Question 3

What did Yaw record? A. Rainfall B. Prices C. Plant growth D. Family names
Answer: C. Plant growth


Question 4

How long did it take the garden to produce? A. One week B. One month C. Three months D. One year
Answer: C. Three months


Question 5

Which crops are mentioned? A. Maize and beans B. Tomatoes and peppers C. Yams and cassava D. Rice and onions
Answer: B. Tomatoes and peppers


Question 6

Who benefited from the garden? A. Several families B. Only Yaw C. Tourists D. Drivers
Answer: A. Several families


Question 7

What is the main idea? A. Gardens are expensive B. Patient work can help a community C. Saturdays are long D. Peppers grow quickly
Answer: B. Patient work can help a community


Question 8

What does “removed weeds” mean? A. Planted weeds B. Took unwanted plants away C. Sold plants D. Counted leaves
Answer: B. Took unwanted plants away


Question 9

What quality did Yaw show? A. Laziness B. Patience C. Anger D. Fear
Answer: B. Patience


Question 10

What can we infer about Yaw? A. He dislikes helping B. He is responsible C. He cannot read D. He wastes food
Answer: B. He is responsible
