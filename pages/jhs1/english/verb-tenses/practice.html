Verb Tenses Practice

JHS 1 English Language

Question 1

She __ to school every day. A. walk B. walks C. walked D. walking
Answer: B. walks


Question 2

They __ football yesterday. A. play B. plays C. played D. playing
Answer: C. played


Question 3

I __ my work tomorrow. A. finish B. finished C. will finish D. finishing
Answer: C. will finish


Question 4

“Now” usually signals the __ tense. A. past B. present continuous C. future D. past perfect
Answer: B. present continuous


Question 5

What is the past of “go”? A. goed B. went C. gone D. going
Answer: B. went


Question 6

We __ eating when he arrived. A. are B. was C. were D. will
Answer: C. were


Question 7

Which is present tense? A. The child sings. B. The child sang. C. The child will sing. D. The child was singing.
Answer: A. The child sings.


Question 8

Choose the correct sentence. A. He have eaten. B. He has eaten. C. He having eat. D. He eat has.
Answer: B. He has eaten.


Question 9

Which clue points to the future? A. last week B. now C. tomorrow D. yesterday
Answer: C. tomorrow


Question 10

What is the past of “see”? A. seed B. saw C. seening D. sees
Answer: B. saw
