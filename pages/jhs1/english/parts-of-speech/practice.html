Parts of Speech Practice

JHS 1 English Language

Question 1

Which word is a noun? A. quickly B. honesty C. jump D. beautiful
Answer: B. honesty


Question 2

Which word is a pronoun? A. they B. market C. red D. sing
Answer: A. they


Question 3

Identify the verb: “The baby smiled.” A. The B. baby C. smiled D. none
Answer: C. smiled


Question 4

Which word is an adjective? A. gently B. tall C. swim D. under
Answer: B. tall


Question 5

Which word is an adverb? A. slowly B. pencil C. blue D. between
Answer: A. slowly


Question 6

Which word is a preposition? A. but B. under C. wow D. farmer
Answer: B. under


Question 7

Which word is a conjunction? A. because B. happy C. loudly D. chair
Answer: A. because


Question 8

Which word is an interjection? A. Hurray! B. carry C. green D. beside
Answer: A. Hurray!


Question 9

In “Ama reads her book,” what is “her”? A. noun B. pronoun C. verb D. adverb
Answer: B. pronoun


Question 10

In “The noisy class laughed,” what is “noisy”? A. adjective B. noun C. verb D. conjunction
Answer: A. adjective
