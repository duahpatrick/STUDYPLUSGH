Family and Relationships Practice

JHS 1 Social Studies

Practice Questions

1. What is a family? Answer: People related by blood, marriage, adoption or care.

2. Name one type of family. Answer: Nuclear or extended family.

3. Who provides guidance in a family? Answer: Parents or guardians.

4. State one duty of a child. Answer: Study, respect elders or help with suitable chores.

5. What is cooperation? Answer: Working together toward a shared goal.

6. Name one quality of a healthy relationship. Answer: Trust, respect or kindness.

7. How should disagreement be handled? Answer: Listen calmly and seek a fair solution.

8. Why share duties fairly? Answer: It reduces burden and builds teamwork.

9. Who belongs to an extended family? Answer: Relatives such as grandparents or cousins.

10. Why is communication important? Answer: It helps people understand needs and solve problems.
