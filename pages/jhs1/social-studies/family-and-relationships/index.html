Family and Relationships

Families support growth, learning and responsible living.

Key idea

Healthy relationships and responsible roles within a family shape good citizenship and personal growth.

What Is a Family?

A family is a group of people related by blood, marriage, adoption or care. A nuclear family usually has parents and children. An extended family includes relatives such as grandparents, uncles and cousins.



Roles and Responsibilities

Parents and guardians provide care, guidance and protection. Children should respect elders, help with suitable chores, study seriously and care for younger members.

Responsibilities should be shared fairly according to age and ability.



Healthy Relationships

Respectful communication, trust, kindness and cooperation strengthen relationships. When disagreement occurs, listen calmly, explain your feelings and seek a fair solution.


Real-life example

A family planning household expenses together is practising cooperation and responsible decision-making.



Remember

Families may look different, but care, respect, protection and cooperation are important in every healthy family.

Quick Check

Which action shows cooperation at home?
