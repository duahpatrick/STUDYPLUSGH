Culture and Heritage

Our heritage connects generations and shapes our identity.

Key idea

Culture and heritage connect people to their history, values, and identity.

Meaning of Culture

Culture is the way of life of a group of people. It includes language, food, dressing, music, dance, festivals, beliefs, values and ways of working.

Culture is learned from family and society, shared by members and passed from one generation to another.



Ghanaian Cultural Diversity

Ghana has many ethnic groups and languages. Diversity gives communities different foods, festivals, crafts and traditions. We should appreciate differences and avoid prejudice.



Heritage and Change

Heritage is what we receive from the past, including monuments, stories, skills and traditional knowledge. Useful traditions should be preserved while harmful practices should be changed through education and law.


Example

Learning a local craft from an older relative preserves practical cultural knowledge.



Remember

Respect all cultures, protect valuable heritage and allow positive ideas to improve community life.
