Culture and Heritage Practice

JHS 1 Social Studies

Practice Questions

1. Define culture. Answer: The way of life of a group.

2. Name two elements of culture. Answer: Language, food, music, beliefs or dress.

3. How is culture learned? Answer: Through family and society.

4. What is heritage? Answer: Valuable things passed from the past.

5. Why is Ghana culturally diverse? Answer: It has many ethnic groups and traditions.

6. Give one cultural festival. Answer: Homowo, Aboakyir, Hogbetsotso or another Ghanaian festival.

7. Why respect other cultures? Answer: Diversity deserves fairness and appreciation.

8. What should happen to harmful practices? Answer: They should change through education and law.

9. How can heritage be preserved? Answer: Teach skills, stories and protect monuments.

10. Is language part of culture? Answer: Yes.
