Citizenship Practice

JHS 1 Social Studies

Practice Questions

1. Who is a citizen? Answer: A legally recognised member of a country.

2. State one citizen right. Answer: Education, safety or fair treatment.

3. State one citizen responsibility. Answer: Obey laws or protect public property.

4. What is democracy? Answer: Government by the people.

5. How can people support democracy? Answer: Participate peacefully and respect opinions.

6. Name one Ghanaian national symbol. Answer: Flag, coat of arms or anthem.

7. Why obey laws? Answer: Laws promote order and protect people.

8. What is public property? Answer: Property provided for everyone’s use.

9. Why should public property be protected? Answer: It serves the whole community.

10. What is responsible freedom? Answer: Using rights without harming others.
