Citizenship

Good citizens help Ghana grow through rights, duties and responsible action.

Key idea

A responsible citizen knows their rights, respects their duties, and helps improve the community.

Who Is a Citizen?

A citizen is a legally recognised member of a country. Citizenship gives a person rights and responsibilities. A good citizen obeys laws, respects others and contributes to community development.



Rights and Responsibilities

Rights include access to education, personal safety, freedom of expression and fair treatment under the law. Responsibilities include obeying laws, paying lawful taxes as an adult, protecting public property and helping the community.

Rights must be exercised responsibly without harming other people.



Democracy and National Symbols

Democracy is government by the people through participation and elected representatives. Voting, peaceful discussion and respecting different opinions support democracy.

The Ghana flag, coat of arms and national anthem are symbols that represent national identity.



Remember

Be truthful, obey fair rules, protect public property and participate peacefully in community life.

Quick Check

Why should citizens protect public property?

It belongs to everyone and supports community services.
