Self and Identity

Knowing yourself helps you make responsible choices.

Key idea

Knowing yourself helps you make wise choices, value your strengths, and respect others.

Meaning of Self and Identity

Self means the total person: thoughts, feelings, abilities and behaviour. Identity is the combination of qualities that makes a person different from others.


Identity may include name, age, family, language, community, interests and beliefs. People can share some qualities and still remain unique.



Self-Esteem and Values

Self-esteem is how a person values and feels about themselves. Healthy self-esteem encourages confidence without looking down on others.

Values are principles that guide behaviour. Honesty, respect, hard work, fairness and responsibility help people live well.



Personal Responsibility

A responsible person accepts the results of their choices, keeps promises, cares for belongings and seeks help when necessary.


Example

If Ama breaks a classroom ruler, she should report it honestly and help replace it instead of blaming a classmate.



Remember

Know your strengths, improve your weaknesses, respect differences and let good values guide your decisions.

Quick Reflection

Which value helps a learner admit a mistake?

Honesty and responsibility.
