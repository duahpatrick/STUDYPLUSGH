Self and Identity Practice

JHS 1 Social Studies

Practice Questions

1. What is identity? Answer: The qualities that make a person unique.

2. What does self mean? Answer: The total person, including thoughts and behaviour.

3. Define self-esteem. Answer: How a person values themselves.

4. State one good value. Answer: Honesty, respect, fairness or hard work.

5. Why respect differences? Answer: People have different qualities and deserve fair treatment.

6. What is responsibility? Answer: Accepting duties and the results of choices.

7. Give one personal strength. Answer: Any genuine ability, such as patience or drawing.

8. What should you do after a mistake? Answer: Admit it and help correct it.

9. How do values guide us? Answer: They help us choose right actions.

10. Why is confidence useful? Answer: It helps a person attempt tasks and make decisions.
