Ghana’s Geography and Resources

Our location, land and resources influence how people live and work.

Location and Physical Features

Ghana is on the west coast of Africa along the Gulf of Guinea. Physical features include mountains, plains, valleys, rivers and lakes. The Volta Lake is an important inland water body.

Climate and relief influence farming, settlement, transport and other activities.



Natural Resources

Natural resources are useful materials provided by nature. Ghana has gold, bauxite, manganese, crude oil, timber, fertile soil, fish and water.

Renewable resources can be replaced naturally, while non-renewable resources such as minerals take a very long time to form.



Responsible Use

Mining and farming provide jobs but can damage land and water if poorly managed. Reclamation, tree planting, regulated fishing and careful water use help protect resources for future generations.


Example

A farmer who prevents bush burning and plants trees is protecting soil and biodiversity.



Remember

Use resources wisely, protect water bodies and land, and balance economic activity with environmental care.
