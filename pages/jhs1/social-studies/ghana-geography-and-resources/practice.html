Ghana’s Geography Practice

JHS 1 Social Studies

Practice Questions

1. On which coast is Ghana? Answer: The west coast of Africa.

2. What body of water borders Ghana? Answer: The Gulf of Guinea.

3. Name one physical feature. Answer: Mountain, plain, valley, river or lake.

4. Name an important inland water body. Answer: Volta Lake.

5. What is a natural resource? Answer: A useful material provided by nature.

6. Name one mineral in Ghana. Answer: Gold, bauxite or manganese.

7. What is renewable resource? Answer: A resource naturally replaced.

8. Is crude oil renewable? Answer: No, it is non-renewable.

9. How can mining damage land? Answer: It can remove soil and pollute water.

10. Give one conservation action. Answer: Tree planting, reclamation or careful water use.
