Environment and Development Practice

JHS 1 Social Studies

Practice Questions

1. What is the environment? Answer: Living and non-living surroundings.

2. Define development. Answer: Positive improvement in quality of life.

3. What is pollution? Answer: Making air, water or land harmful.

4. Name one cause of pollution. Answer: Littering, illegal mining or poor waste disposal.

5. State one effect of deforestation. Answer: Soil loss, flooding or habitat loss.

6. What is sustainable development? Answer: Meeting present needs without harming future needs.

7. What does reduce mean? Answer: Use less of a resource or create less waste.

8. Give one way to conserve water. Answer: Close taps and repair leaks.

9. Why plant trees? Answer: Trees protect soil, air and habitats.

10. Name one community environmental action. Answer: Organised clean-up or tree planting.
