Environment and Development

Healthy surroundings support healthy people and lasting progress.

Key idea

Balanced development protects the environment and supports long-term community well-being.

Our Environment

The environment includes living things, non-living things and the relationships between them. Air, water, soil, plants, animals and people all form part of our surroundings.

Development means positive improvement in people’s quality of life, including education, health, jobs and safe infrastructure.



Environmental Problems

Pollution makes air, water or land harmful. Littering, illegal mining, deforestation, noise and improper waste disposal can cause disease, flooding, soil loss and loss of habitats.



Sustainable Development

Sustainable development meets present needs without destroying the ability of future generations to meet theirs. Reduce waste, reuse useful items, recycle materials, conserve water and plant trees.


Community action

A clean-up exercise, proper waste bins and education can reduce litter around a school or market.



Remember

Everyone has a role in keeping the environment clean and using resources carefully.

Quick Check

Name one action that can reduce deforestation.

Planting trees or using forest resources responsibly.
