Human Body and Health

Learn how body systems work and how to protect your health.

Key idea

The body works as a system, and healthy habits help protect growth, energy, and wellbeing.

Body Systems

The digestive system breaks food into usable nutrients. The respiratory system exchanges oxygen and carbon dioxide. The circulatory system transports substances in blood. The excretory system removes wastes.


Worked example

During exercise, muscles need more oxygen. Breathing and heart rates increase so oxygen reaches cells faster.



Balanced Diet

A balanced diet contains carbohydrates for energy, proteins for growth and repair, fats and oils for stored energy, vitamins and minerals for protection, fibre for digestion and water for body processes.


Local foods such as beans, fish, kontomire, fruits, cereals and clean water can contribute to a balanced diet.



Hygiene and Disease Prevention

Wash hands with soap, use safe water, keep food covered, dispose of waste properly and keep surroundings clean. Vaccination and mosquito-net use help prevent some diseases.


Malaria is spread by infected female Anopheles mosquitoes; it is not spread by simply sharing food.



Remember

Healthy choices combine nutritious food, exercise, rest, hygiene and prompt medical care.
