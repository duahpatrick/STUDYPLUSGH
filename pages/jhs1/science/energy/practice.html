Energy Practice

JHS 1 Integrated Science

What is energy?

Answer: The ability to do work or cause change


2

What energy does a moving object have?

Answer: Kinetic energy


3

What energy is stored in food?

Answer: Chemical energy


4

Give one renewable energy source.

Answer: Sunlight


5

Give one non-renewable energy source.

Answer: Coal


6

What energy does a stretched rubber band store?

Answer: Potential energy


7

What useful energy does a torch produce?

Answer: Light energy


8

What happens to energy in a conversion?

Answer: It changes form


9

State one way to save electricity.

Answer: Switch off unused lights


10

What unwanted energy can a machine produce?

Answer: Heat or sound
