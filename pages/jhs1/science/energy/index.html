Energy

Understand energy forms, sources and useful transformations.

Key idea

Energy makes things happen, and it can be stored, transferred, and transformed in many ways.

Forms of Energy

Common forms include light, heat, sound, electrical, chemical, kinetic and potential energy. Energy is the ability to do work or cause change.


Example

Food stores chemical energy. Your body changes it into movement and heat when you run.



Energy Transfer

Energy moves from one object or place to another. In a torch, chemical energy in the battery becomes electrical energy, then light and heat energy.


Energy is not created or destroyed; it changes from one form to another.



Sources and Conservation

Renewable sources include sunlight, wind and flowing water. Non-renewable fuels such as coal, oil and natural gas are limited and can cause pollution.


Save energy by switching off unused lights, using efficient appliances and avoiding waste.



Remember

Every energy conversion may produce some unwanted heat or sound.
