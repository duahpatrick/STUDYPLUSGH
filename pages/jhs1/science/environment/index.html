The Environment

Learn how organisms interact with one another and their surroundings.

Key idea

Healthy environments support life, and caring for them helps people and communities thrive.

Environment and Ecosystems

The environment includes living factors, such as plants, animals and microbes, and non-living factors, such as air, water, soil, light and temperature.


An ecosystem is a community of organisms interacting with one another and with their physical surroundings.



Food Chains

Green plants are producers because they make food. Herbivores eat plants, carnivores eat animals and decomposers break down dead material.


Worked example

Grass → grasshopper → frog → snake shows the direction of food and energy transfer. The arrow means “is eaten by”.



Pollution and Conservation

Pollution is the harmful addition of substances to the environment. Smoke pollutes air; plastics can pollute land and water; noise affects people and animals.


Reduce, reuse and recycle. Plant trees, protect water bodies and dispose of waste responsibly.



Remember

Human activities can damage ecosystems, but wise choices can conserve natural resources.
