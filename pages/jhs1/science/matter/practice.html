What is matter?

Answer: Anything with mass and volume


2

Which state has a fixed shape and volume?

Answer: Solid


3

Which state takes the shape of its container?

Answer: Liquid


4

Answer: Gas


5

What is solid changing to liquid called?

Answer: Melting


6

Answer: Evaporation


7

What is gas changing to liquid called?

Answer: Condensation


8

Is cutting paper physical or chemical?

Answer: Physical change

Answer: Iron oxide
