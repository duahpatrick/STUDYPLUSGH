Measurement Practice

JHS 1 Mathematics

Question 1

How many centimetres are in 3 metres?

A. 30

B. 300

C. 3,000

D. 0.3

Answer: B. 300



Question 2

Which unit is best for measuring the mass of a bag of rice?

A. Millimetre

B. Litre

C. Kilogram

D. Centimetre

Answer: C. Kilogram



Question 3

Find the perimeter of a square with side 6 cm.

A. 12 cm

B. 18 cm

C. 24 cm

D. 36 cm

Answer: C. 24 cm



Question 4

Find the area of a rectangle 7 cm by 4 cm.

A. 11 cm²

B. 22 cm²

C. 28 cm²

D. 49 cm²

Answer: C. 28 cm²



Question 5

How many grams are in 2 kilograms?

A. 20

B. 200

C. 2,000

D. 20,000

Answer: C. 2,000



Question 6

How many minutes are in 2 hours?

A. 60

B. 100

C. 120

D. 240

Answer: C. 120



Question 7

What is the perimeter of a rectangle 10 m long and 3 m wide?

A. 13 m

B. 20 m

C. 26 m

D. 30 m

Answer: C. 26 m



Question 8

Which unit measures liquid capacity?

A. Litre

B. Metre

C. Gram

D. Square metre

Answer: A. Litre



Question 9

What is 450 cm in metres?

A. 0.45 m

B. 4.5 m

C. 45 m

D. 450 m

Answer: B. 4.5 m



Question 10

A lesson begins at 8:00 a.m. and ends at 9:30 a.m. How long is it?

A. 30 minutes

B. 1 hour

C. 1 hour 30 minutes

D. 2 hours

Answer: C. 1 hour 30 minutes
