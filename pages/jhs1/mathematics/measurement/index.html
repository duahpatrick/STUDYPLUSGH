JHS 1 Mathematics

Measurement

Measure length, mass, time, perimeter, area and volume accurately.

Key idea

Choosing the right unit and converting carefully makes measurement clear and useful in real life.

Units and Measuring Tools

Measurement compares an object with a standard unit. Length is measured in millimetres, centimetres, metres and kilometres. Use a ruler for short lengths, a tape measure for longer lengths, and a measuring wheel for distances.

Mass is measured in grams and kilograms. Capacity is measured in millilitres and litres. Choose a unit that suits the object.

Converting Units

1 metre = 100 centimetres, 1 centimetre = 10 millimetres, 1 kilometre = 1,000 metres, 1 kilogram = 1,000 grams and 1 litre = 1,000 millilitres.

Example: 3.5 m = 350 cm

Multiply by 100 because metres are larger units than centimetres.

Perimeter and Area

Perimeter is the distance around a shape. For a rectangle, perimeter = 2 x (length + width). Area is the surface covered by a shape. Rectangle area = length x width and square area = side x side.

Always write square units for area, such as cm², and ordinary units for perimeter, such as cm.

Time and Temperature

There are 60 seconds in a minute, 60 minutes in an hour and 24 hours in a day. To change hours to minutes, multiply by 60. To change minutes to hours, divide by 60.

Temperature is measured in degrees Celsius (°C). Read the scale carefully and include the unit.

Worked Example

A rectangular garden is 8 m long and 5 m wide. Find its perimeter and area.

Perimeter = 2 x (8 + 5) = 26 m. Area = 8 x 5 = 40 m².
