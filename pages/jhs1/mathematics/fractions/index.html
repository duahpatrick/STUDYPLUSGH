JHS 1 Fractions

Learn how to read, compare and work with fractions in everyday life.

Key idea

A fraction tells us how many equal parts of a whole we are considering.

What Is a Fraction?

A fraction represents part of a whole. In 3/5, 3 is the numerator and 5 is the denominator.

The denominator shows the total number of equal parts. The numerator shows how many parts are being considered.

Types of Fractions

A proper fraction has a numerator smaller than its denominator, such as 2/7.

An improper fraction has a numerator equal to or greater than its denominator, such as 9/4.

A mixed number combines a whole number and a proper fraction, such as 2 1/4.

Equivalent Fractions

Equivalent fractions have the same value. Multiplying or dividing both numerator and denominator by the same non-zero number gives an equivalent fraction.

1/2 = 2/4 = 3/6

Comparing Fractions

Fractions with the same denominator can be compared by comparing their numerators.

5/8 > 3/8

To compare fractions with different denominators, find equivalent fractions with a common denominator.

Mini Check
