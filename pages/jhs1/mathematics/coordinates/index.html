Coordinates

Locate points and describe movement on a coordinate grid.

Key idea

Every point on the grid is identified by an ordered pair, and moving across and up or down changes its position.

The Coordinate Plane

A coordinate grid has a horizontal x-axis and a vertical y-axis. They meet at the origin, (0, 0).

The axes divide the plane into four quadrants. Positive x moves to the right and positive y moves upward.

Ordered Pairs

A point is written as (x, y). Read across first to find x, then move up or down to find y.

Point A(3, 2): move 3 units right and 2 units up.

Plotting and Reading Points

To plot (-2, 4), begin at the origin, move 2 units left because x is negative, then 4 units up because y is positive.

A point such as (5, -1) is 5 right and 1 down.

Distance and Symmetry

When points share the same horizontal line, count the difference between their x-coordinates.

Reflection in the x-axis changes (x, y) to (x, -y). Reflection in the y-axis changes it to (-x, y).

Movement on a Grid

Translations move a shape without turning it. “3 right and 2 up” means add (3, 2) to every point.

Worked Example

Point B is (-1, 3). What is its reflection in the x-axis?

The x-coordinate stays -1 and the y-coordinate changes sign: B' = (-1, -3).

Quick Check

Write the ordered pair for 4 units left and 2 units down from the origin.
