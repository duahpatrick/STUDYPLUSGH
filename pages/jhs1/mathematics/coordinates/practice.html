Coordinates Practice

JHS 1 Mathematics

Question 1

What is the origin?

A. (1, 1)

B. (0, 0)

C. (0, 1)

D. (1, 0)

Answer: B. (0, 0)



Question 2

In (4, 2), what is the x-coordinate?

A. 2

B. 4

C. 6

D. -4

Answer: B. 4



Question 3

In which direction is positive y?

A. Left

B. Right

C. Up

D. Down

Answer: C. Up



Question 4

Which point is 3 left and 2 up?

A. (3, 2)

B. (-3, 2)

C. (3, -2)

D. (-2, 3)

Answer: B. (-3, 2)



Question 5

Reflect (5, 2) in the x-axis.

A. (-5, 2)

B. (5, -2)

C. (-5, -2)

D. (2, 5)

Answer: B. (5, -2)



Question 6

Which point lies on the y-axis?

A. (4, 0)

B. (0, 4)

C. (4, 4)

D. (-4, 4)

Answer: B. (0, 4)



Question 7

How far apart are (2, 3) and (7, 3)?

A. 3 units

B. 5 units

C. 7 units

D. 10 units

Answer: B. 5 units



Question 8

What is the y-coordinate of (-2, -6)?

A. -2

B. 2

C. -6

D. 6

Answer: C. -6



Question 9

Move (1, 2) 3 right. What is the new point?

A. (4, 2)

B. (1, 5)

C. (-2, 2)

D. (3, 4)

Answer: A. (4, 2)



Question 10

Which point is in the lower-left direction from the origin?

A. (2, 3)

B. (-2, 3)

C. (2, -3)

D. (-2, -3)

Answer: D. (-2, -3)
