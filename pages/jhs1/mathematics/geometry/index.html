JHS 1 Geometry

Explore lines, angles, shapes and their properties.

Key idea

Geometry helps us describe the size, shape, position and relationship of objects around us.

Lines and Line Segments

A line extends forever in both directions. A line segment has two endpoints and a fixed length. A ray has one endpoint and extends forever in one direction.

Parallel lines never meet, while perpendicular lines meet at a right angle of 90 degrees.

Angles

An angle is formed when two rays meet at a common endpoint called the vertex.

Acute angles are less than 90 degrees. A right angle is exactly 90 degrees. An obtuse angle is greater than 90 degrees but less than 180 degrees. A straight angle is 180 degrees.

Angles on a straight line add up to 180 degrees.

Triangles

A triangle has three sides and three angles. The angles in every triangle add up to 180 degrees.

An equilateral triangle has three equal sides. An isosceles triangle has two equal sides. A scalene triangle has no equal sides.

Missing angle = 180 - the sum of the two known angles.

Quadrilaterals

A quadrilateral has four sides and four angles. The angles in every quadrilateral add up to 360 degrees.

A square has four equal sides and four right angles. A rectangle has opposite sides equal and four right angles. A parallelogram has two pairs of parallel sides.

Perimeter

Perimeter is the distance around the outside of a shape. Add all the side lengths to find it.

For a rectangle, perimeter = 2 x (length + width). For a square, perimeter = 4 x side length.

Symmetry

A line of symmetry divides a shape into two matching halves. A square has four lines of symmetry, while a rectangle has two.

Worked Example: Rectangle

A rectangle is 8 cm long and 5 cm wide. Find its perimeter.

Perimeter = 2 x (8 + 5) = 2 x 13 = 26 cm.

Quick Check

A triangle has angles of 50 degrees and 60 degrees. What is the third angle?
