Question 1

How many sides does a triangle have?


A. 2

B. 3

C. 4

D. 5


Answer: B. 3



Question 2

What is the size of a right angle?


A. 45 degrees

B. 90 degrees

C. 180 degrees

D. 360 degrees


Answer: B. 90 degrees



Question 3

What is the sum of angles in a triangle?


A. 90 degrees

B. 180 degrees

C. 270 degrees

D. 360 degrees


Answer: B. 180 degrees



Question 4

Which triangle has three equal sides?


A. Scalene

B. Isosceles

C. Equilateral

D. Right-angled


Answer: C. Equilateral



Question 5

What is the sum of angles in a quadrilateral?


A. 180 degrees

B. 270 degrees

C. 360 degrees

D. 540 degrees


Answer: C. 360 degrees



Question 6

Find the perimeter of a square with side 6 cm.


A. 12 cm

B. 18 cm

C. 24 cm

D. 36 cm


Answer: C. 24 cm



Question 7

Find the perimeter of a rectangle 7 cm long and 3 cm wide.


A. 10 cm

B. 17 cm

C. 20 cm

D. 21 cm


Answer: C. 20 cm



Question 8

Two lines that never meet are called what?


A. Perpendicular

B. Parallel

C. Curved

D. Intersecting


Answer: B. Parallel



Question 9

A. 40 degrees

B. 50 degrees

C. 60 degrees

D. 80 degrees


Answer: C. 60 degrees
