Ratio and Proportion

Understand comparisons, equivalent ratios and real-life problems step by step.

Key idea

A ratio compares quantities, and proportion tells us when two comparisons are equal.

Meaning of Ratio

A ratio compares two or more quantities of the same kind. It tells us how much of one quantity there is compared with another.

Example: 3 red balls and 2 blue balls → 3:2

The order matters: blue balls to red balls is 2:3. Ratios can also be written as 3 to 2 or 3/2.

Simplifying Ratios

To simplify a ratio, divide every part by their greatest common factor.

12:18 = 2:3

Both 12 and 18 are divided by 6. Never divide only one part of a ratio.

Equivalent Ratios

Equivalent ratios describe the same comparison. Multiplying or dividing every part by the same non-zero number keeps the ratio equivalent.

2:5 = 4:10 = 6:15

Sharing in a Given Ratio

To share an amount in the ratio 2:3, first add the parts: 2 + 3 = 5. Divide the total by 5 to find one part, then multiply by 2 and 3.

Share 40 cedis in the ratio 2:3 → 16 cedis and 24 cedis

Proportion

A proportion states that two ratios are equal. In 2:3 = 8:12, both ratios simplify to the same comparison.

To find an unknown in a proportion, use cross multiplication.

3/5 = x/20 → x = 12

Worked Example: Unit Rate

A taxi travels 180 kilometres in 3 hours. What is its average distance per hour?

Average rate = distance / time = 180 / 3 = 60 kilometres per hour.
