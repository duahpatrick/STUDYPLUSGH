Ratio and Proportion Practice

JHS 1 Mathematics

Question 1

Write the ratio of 4 cats to 7 dogs.


A. 7:4

B. 4:7

C. 4:11

D. 11:4


Answer: B. 4:7



Question 2

Simplify 18:24.


A. 2:3

B. 3:4

C. 4:3

D. 6:8


Answer: B. 3:4



Question 3

Which ratio is equivalent to 5:2?


A. 10:4

B. 15:8

C. 20:10

D. 25:12


Answer: A. 10:4



Question 4

There are 3 boys and 5 girls. What is the ratio of girls to boys?


A. 3:5

B. 5:3

C. 8:3

D. 3:8


Answer: B. 5:3



Question 5

Share 24 in the ratio 1:3. What is the smaller share?


A. 4

B. 6

C. 8

D. 18


Answer: B. 6



Question 6

Share 45 cedis in the ratio 2:3. What is the larger share?


A. 18

B. 20

C. 27

D. 30


Answer: C. 27



Question 7

If 2 pencils cost 6 cedis, how much does 1 pencil cost?


A. 2 cedis

B. 3 cedis

C. 4 cedis

D. 8 cedis


Answer: B. 3 cedis



Question 8

If 3/4 = x/20, find x.


A. 12

B. 15

C. 16

D. 18


Answer: B. 15



Question 9

A car travels 120 km in 2 hours. What is its average speed?


A. 30 km/h

B. 60 km/h

C. 120 km/h

D. 240 km/h


Answer: B. 60 km/h



Question 10

Complete the proportion: 4:5 = 12:__.


A. 10

B. 12

C. 15

D. 20


Answer: C. 15
