Grammar Revision

Understand sentence building, word classes and proper usage.

1. Nouns

A noun names a person, place, animal or thing.

  • Person: teacher, doctor
  • Place: school, market
  • Thing: book, desk
  • Animal: goat, bird


2. Verbs

A verb shows action or state of being.

Examples: run, sing, is, are, write, learn.

In the sentence “The pupils write neatly,” the verb is write.



3. Adjectives

An adjective describes a noun.

Examples: tall, bright, careful, handsome.

“The bright student answered correctly.” Here, bright describes the student.



4. Tense

Tense tells us when an action happens.

  • Present: I read every evening.
  • Past: I read yesterday.
  • Future: I will read tomorrow.


Worked Example

Complete the sentence: “The children ______ the football.”

The correct verb is “kick.” So the sentence is: “The children kick the football.”
