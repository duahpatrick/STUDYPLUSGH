Geometry Revision

Learn about angles, shapes, triangles and the rules that connect them.

1. Angles

An angle is formed when two rays meet at a point called the vertex.

  • Acute angle: less than 90°
  • Right angle: exactly 90°
  • Obtuse angle: between 90° and 180°
  • Straight angle: 180°


2. Angles on a Straight Line

Angles on a straight line add up to 180°.

If one angle is 75°, the other is 180° - 75° = 105°.



3. Triangles

The sum of the interior angles of a triangle is always 180°.

  • Equilateral triangle: all sides and angles are equal
  • Isosceles triangle: two equal sides and two equal angles
  • Scalene triangle: no equal sides


4. Quadrilaterals

The sum of the interior angles of a quadrilateral is 360°.

Examples include squares, rectangles, parallelograms and trapeziums.



Worked Example

A triangle has angles 40° and 65°. Find the third angle.

Sum of angles in a triangle = 180°
180° - 40° - 65° = 75°
Third angle = 75°.



Quick Check

A right-angled triangle has one angle of 35°. What is the third angle?
